## Permits and Engineering Are Handled In-House

Here's where most ADU projects fall apart — and where most homeowners don't realize the risk until it's too late.

With a typical contractor, the process works like this:
1. You hire a contractor to build the ADU
2. The contractor sends you to a separate architect to draw plans
3. The architect sends you to a separate engineer for structural and Title 24 calcs
4. You submit plans to the city yourself (or the architect does, eventually)
5. The city comes back with corrections
6. You go back to the architect, who goes back to the engineer
7. Weeks pass. Months pass. You haven't broken ground.

Every handoff between companies is a delay. Every miscommunication between the architect, engineer, and contractor is a change order. Every correction from the city means going back through the chain.

IMKAT eliminates this entirely. Design, engineering, and permitting all happen under one roof. Their in-house team handles:

- Architectural design and floor plans
- Structural engineering
- Title 24 energy compliance
- City permit applications and follow-up
- Plan corrections and resubmissions

When the city flags something on the plans, IMKAT's engineer fixes it the same day — not two weeks later after three emails between separate companies. When a design decision affects the structural plan, the architect and engineer are sitting in the same office, not trading redlines across town.

The result: faster permits, fewer surprises, and a build that starts on schedule. For homeowners, it means one point of contact instead of three separate companies who each point at the other when something goes wrong.
